🏠 **1. Smart Home IoT Automation & Real-Time Telemetry**
- Live telemetry stream simulating ESP32 microcontrollers (ACS712 current sensors, YF-S201 flow meters, MQ-135 air sensors).
- Real-time monitoring of Solar Edge 5kW Inverters, living room smart AC loads, and EV chargers.
- **AI Behavioral Energy Nudges**: Proactive alerts (e.g., shifting thermostat +1°C saves ~4.2 kWh/day and boosts GreenScore by +2.4 pts).

🎓 **2. University Campus Sustainability**
- Real-time departmental rankings (CS #1: 91.2, Math #2: 87.5, Physics #3: 84.1).
- Aggregates 142.5 MWh solar generation, paperless exam drives, and inter-faculty eco duels.

🏢 **3. Enterprise ESG Decarbonization & Scope 1, 2, 3 Accounting**
- Full GHG Protocol carbon accounting across Scope 1 (Direct), Scope 2 (Purchased Electricity), and Scope 3 (Supply Chain).
- Departmental decarbonization matrix and executive insight cards.

📄 **4. Automated GRI & ISSB ESG Disclosure Engine**
- One-click corporate sustainability audit reporting compliant with GRI 302/305 and ISSB S2 standards with JSON/CSV/PDF export.

🏙️ **5. Municipal Citywide Sustainability Grid**
- City Index (78.4 / 100) tracking municipal energy grids (24% solar), water recovery, EV fast-charging hubs (184 active hubs), and real-time Air Quality Index (AQI 42 Good).

🔮 **6. 2030 Climate Scenario AI Engine**
- Interactive policy sandbox (Renewable %, EV penetration %, Reforestation %) simulating 2030 Earth temperature anomalies and economic returns.

👁️ **7. AR Spatial Thermal Heatmap Scanner**
- HTML5 Canvas spatial renderer detecting indoor AC door seal cold leaks and rooftop solar panel hotspot irradiance.

💎 **8. Cryptographic Carbon Credit NFT Ledger**
- Verra VCS & Gold Standard certified carbon credit minting with immutable cryptographic audit hashes (0x7f9a...).

📡 **9. Developer API Sandbox & Voice AI**
- Live Swagger-style API key generator (`gs_live_...`), endpoint test runner, and hands-free voice synthesis command center.

🎨 **10. Dual Light & Dark Theme Engine**
- Seamless toggle between Cyber Emerald Glassmorphism (Dark) and Fresh Pure Mint (Light) modes.
